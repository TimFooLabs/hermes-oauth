Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Application: Hermes Agent (Personal), owned by Timothy Foo (timothyfoo@gmail.com).

This application is a single-user, personal automation tool. This policy explains what happens to data when the owner authorizes it with his own Google account.

What the app accesses

With the owner's explicit OAuth consent, the app may access the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Docs and Contacts, solely to perform tasks the owner requests (for example reading a specific email thread or preparing a draft for his review).

Where data is stored

All data the app processes stays on the owner's own computers. OAuth credentials are stored locally on his machines and are never shared with anyone. The app runs no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking, and does not sell or transfer data to any third party.

Other users

The app is not available to other users. No data is collected from anyone other than the owner's own accounts.
