Hermes Agent (Personal)

This is a personal automation application owned and operated by Timothy Foo. It uses Google OAuth (desktop application) so that his own agent software can, at his direction and only for his own accounts, read and send email, and access his own Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs and Contacts.

It is not distributed to other users, collects no data from third parties, and has no advertising, analytics or third-party trackers.

Owner: Timothy Foo · Support: timothyfoo@gmail.com · Privacy policy
